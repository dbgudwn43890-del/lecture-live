# Lecue — US launch ad, revision 2

**AI that listens with you.**

This package supersedes the Korean, subject-specific concept in `lecue-youtube-ad-2026-09-11`. The intended audience is US college and graduate students. All finished-ad copy, narration, captions, UI labels, and disclosures must be in US English.

The ad shows a use: Lecue is listening, the student types a short question, a rich visual answer unfolds, and recording continues. The viewer never has to read or understand lecture material. No source lecture content appears. The answer is visible and beautifully structured: graph, short takeaway, and two explanation steps.

## Files

- [01-creative-plan.md](01-creative-plan.md): strategy, timed storyboard, art direction, sound, cutdowns, handoff.
- [02-claude-production-prompt.md](02-claude-production-prompt.md): English execution prompt for Claude and connected production tools.
- [03-shotlist.json](03-shotlist.json): structured timing and copy.
- [04-copy.txt](04-copy.txt): locked US-English copy and narration.
- [05-evidence-and-assets.md](05-evidence-and-assets.md): actual product labels, evidence, and capture boundaries.
- [06-visual-answer-fixture.json](06-visual-answer-fixture.json): synthetic chart example grounded in supplied test data.
- [subtitles](subtitles): draft 30-, 15-, and 6-second English SRT files; retime against final audio.
- [assets](assets): copies of approved brand artwork. Product screenshots are not included.

These are production instructions and a storyboard, not finished videos or measured product-performance footage. The earlier product research remains applicable; this revision changes the creative, language, audience, and content treatment.

Recommended handoff: give Claude this entire package and use `02-claude-production-prompt.md` as the task. Confirm the actual connected tool capabilities at execution time. No model-specific API is assumed.
